    Rules bench

    Change a rule, restart, play it. Nothing here is written back to the project — when a setting earns its place, copy the number into js/core/config.js yourself.

    Board
    Columns
    6
    Rows
    6

    Twelve rows collides with the hand on a 375px phone. Space buys moves, not rungs: tripling the board was worth about 1.5 tiers.

    Merging
    Pieces per merge
    3

    The exponent the whole ladder rides on: at 3 a rung costs ~27 dealt pieces, at 4 about 81. Three is the floor — two collapses the board on contact, so it is off the dial. Raising it makes the climb much steeper, not a little.

    Corners count as touching

    Diagonals as well as the four sides. Changes how the board feels, but not how high you climb — adjacency was never the thing holding the ladder back. Rubble, dynamite and the star still read the four sides only.

    Deal behind your best
    1

    How far below your best piece the hand is drawn from. Second strongest lever: 2 → 1 was worth five tiers. At 0 games get short and brittle.

    Falling
    Pieces off each fall
    1
    Extra plays between falls
    1

    Together these ran 17% longer games with the score intact. Past +1 spacing it stops paying.

    Dials
    Merges to fill the star
    60

    Left of the hand. Counted in merges, not points: points inflate up the ladder and a late cascade pays several at once, which had stars arriving every 22 moves early and every 6 late. Counted this way it lands every ~40 moves wherever you are. Press it, then tap a piece: every one of that kind leaves. A sweep costs a turn.

    Fallen pieces per bomb
    10

    Right of the hand. Fills on what the sky drops on you, not on scoring — so the worse a run is going, the sooner you are handed the thing that digs you out. Press it, then tap a column: a stick drops in and burns down there. Free — it would be a poor gift if the sky got a fall out of it too.

    Turns before a stick blows
    5

    A stick goes off on its own after this many turns, or sooner if a merge beside it lights it. Dynamite never falls from the sky now — the only sticks on the board are the ones you placed.
